## Episode 1: The Ash Chalice

**INT. BLOODMOON PACK GREAT HALL - NIGHT**

Towering obsidian pillars frame a grand altar bathed in crimson light from a blood-red full moon overhead. Hundreds of Bloodmoon Pack members watch in breathless silence.

At the base of the stone dais kneels LYRA VANCE (18), gaunt, barefoot, clad in a torn charcoal-gray burlap tunic stained with grey kitchen ash. A worn bronze pendant hangs on a leather cord around her throat.

A heavy bronze bell chimes midnight.

EXTREME CLOSE-UP on Lyra's bruised left wrist. A searing golden-red wolf-rune flares across her skin—identical to the glowing mark erupting on the right wrist of ALPHA KAELEN THORNE (22) atop the black-and-gold throne.

Lyra's dark obsidian eyes widen with sudden, trembling hope.

LYRA
Kaelen... the mark—

*CRASH!*

Kaelen's gauntleted fist slams down onto the ceremonial table, shattering the golden mating chalice into jagged shards at Lyra's bare feet. Ceremonial red wine splashes across her ankles like blood.

Gasps ripple through the hall. Beside the throne stands SELENE VANCE (20) in an opulent ivory gown. At Selene's throat rests the *Tear of the Bloodwood*—a blue-silver moonstone radiating a borrowed white halo.

Kaelen rises to his full six-foot-three height, amber Alpha eyes pitiless as he steps down the dais.

KAELEN
The Bloodmoon throne does not mate with hollow-blooded kitchen filth. Drink the ash and crawl back to the dark, Lyra—your sister is my Luna.

Selene presses a manicured hand to her chest, offering the crowd a practiced, tearful sigh.

SELENE
Oh, Lyra... the Moon Goddess must have made a terrible mistake marking a girl without a wolf. If your empty veins taint Kaelen's Alpha blood, who will save our pack from the Crimson Blight?

HIGH PRIEST MALAKOR (55), skeletal in towering crimson-and-gold robes, steps forward carrying a black iron bowl of smoking silver-black embers.

MALAKOR
Only Bond-Severing Ash can burn a defective mark from the Alpha's bloodline before the moon sets! Drink, servant, and release your betters!

Lyra scrambles back half a step on the cold stone, her left hand clutching the bronze pendant at her throat. She looks straight past the priest into Kaelen's eyes.

LYRA
Look at me, Kaelen! Ten years ago in the Great Frost, you swore a blood oath to the girl who fed you her blood in the snowdrift. If you sever this bond tonight, you sever the only thing keeping your pack alive!

Kaelen's jaw tightens. He reaches back, resting his hand beneath the glowing moonstone at Selene's throat.

KAELEN
Selene bore the *Tear of the Bloodwood* through that blizzard while you hid in the cellars. Keep my savior's miracle out of your lying mouth.

Kaelen snaps his fingers.

KAELEN (CONT'D)
Hold her.

Two massive PACK ENFORCERS lunging from the shadows wrench Lyra's bruised arms behind her back, forcing her to her knees and prying her jaw open.

Kaelen takes the black iron bowl from Malakor himself. Stepping over the shattered golden chalice, he pours the glowing, searing Bond-Severing Ash straight down Lyra's throat.

LYRA
*Aaaagh!*

Scorching black fire erupts beneath the skin of Lyra's neck. The golden-red mate mark on her left wrist fractures like burning glass.

Released by the guards, Lyra collapses forward onto the stone altar, coughing dark crimson blood onto the obsidian floor.

## Episode 2: Over the Bone Cliff

**INT. BLOODMOON PACK GREAT HALL - NIGHT (CONTINUOUS)**

Lyra writhes on the stone altar, black-veined fire ripping through her chest as crimson blood drips from her chin.

*CRACK.*

Selene's silver stiletto heel slams down onto Lyra's bruised left hand, snapping the leather cord of her mother's bronze pendant and grinding the metal casing into cracked fragments against the stone.

LYRA
No—my mother's—!

Selene crouches gracefully beside her, angling her ivory silk shoulders to block the crowd's view. Her saintly pout vanishes into a venomous, gleeful sneer.

SELENE
(whispering)
Oh, sister, don't look at Kaelen like that. A mute little gutter-rat doesn't need a mother's trinket—or a mate. Wait until you're rotting at the bottom of Bone Cliff; I'll tell him how you begged.

Halfway up the dais, Kaelen suddenly gasps. He clenches his fist over his sternum—right over an old, faded frost-fern scar—as a sharp, inexplicable cardiac pain lances through his chest.

Shaking off the tremor, Kaelen hardens his expression and looks down at Lyra.

KAELEN
The bond is broken. Kneel and wish your Luna well, Lyra, and I will let you live out your days in the kennels.

The pack jeers from the galleries. An Enforcer kicks the back of Lyra's knee to force her forehead to the floor.

Lyra's fingers close tightly around the crushed bronze shards of her mother's pendant, the sharp metal slicing into her left palm. The last flicker of warmth in her obsidian eyes dies, replaced by terrifying, glacial stillness.

Bracing her spine like iron, Lyra forces herself upright against the Enforcer's grip until she is standing straight before the dais.

She looks past Selene and spits a mouthful of dark crimson blood directly onto Kaelen's gold-plated boots.

LYRA
Pour the ash down my throat, Kaelen. Carve my name off your pack stones. When the sky turns silver, you will crawl across glass just to kiss the hem of my cloak.

Dead silence drops over the hall. Beside the altar, High Priest Malakor's sunken yellow eyes widen in sudden, recognizing terror at the words *when the sky turns silver*.

MALAKOR
Heresy! Alpha, her blood is cursed—cast her into the gorge before she defiles the wedding!

Furious at the blood on his boots and the eerie chill in his chest, Kaelen waves a dismissive hand.

KAELEN
Throw the defective trash off Bone Cliff.

**EXT. THE BONE CLIFF EDGE - NIGHT**

Jagged black rock overhangs a bottomless abyss of boiling storm clouds and forked lightning.

Two Enforcers swing Lyra's limp, blood-soaked body to the precipice and hurl her over the edge.

Lyra plummets backward into the roaring darkness, wind whipping her raven hair across her pale face. As the abyss swallows her, her bleeding left palm presses hard into the cracked bronze pendant against her sternum—and a tiny spark of blinding white-silver fire ignites inside the metal.

## Episode 3: Crowned in Silver Flame

**EXT. BASE OF THE BONE CLIFF - NIGHT**

Lyra's broken body lies motionless on jagged obsidian rocks at the bottom of the gorge. Dark blood pools beneath her ribs.

High overhead, the blood-red full moon suddenly cracks down the center like a shattered ruby. Blinding, apocalyptic white-silver light floods the entire night sky.

**INT. BLOODMOON BANQUET HALL - NIGHT (INTERCUT)**

Golden chandeliers blaze above long obsidian tables. Kaelen stands beside Selene on the high dais, raising a ceremonial goblet.

KAELEN
To the Bloodmoon Pack, and to the Luna whose light will cure our land—

Outside the towering stained-glass windows, the crimson sky turns incandescent silver. Kaelen freezes mid-toast, his heart hammering violently against his ribs.

**EXT. BASE OF THE BONE CLIFF - NIGHT**

Down in the gorge, the black Bond-Severing Ash burning inside Lyra's veins collides with the silver spark inside her mother's crushed bronze pendant.

Instead of killing her, the lethal shockwave shatters an ancient, invisible blood-seal around her heart. The bronze fragments melt into liquid mercury, sinking straight through the skin of her chest.

SERAPHINA (V.O.)
(echoing, ethereal whisper)
*When the mortal seal breaks, my daughter... the wolves you endured will bow to your flame.*

Lyra's eyes snap open—no longer dark obsidian, but blazing liquid mercury-silver.

A roaring vertical pillar of silver moonfire erupts from the gorge floor, lifting Lyra's body into the air. Her blood-stained burlap rags disintegrate in the celestial heat, weaving themselves into a high-collared cloak of midnight shadow-silk.

At the center of her forehead, a luminous silver crescent brands itself into her skin. Simultaneously, **four jagged black-crimson lines—the Four Vein-Locks—**sear across her left collarbone, pointing like dark daggers toward her heart.

Lyra's bare feet touch down on the jagged stone. Instantly, a delicate, glowing **silver frost-fern pattern** crystallizes across the rock beneath her toes.

She looks up toward the distant citadel lights and exhales a single, quiet word:

LYRA
Kneel.

**INT. BLOODMOON BANQUET HALL - NIGHT**

*BOOM!*

A visible ring of silver shockwave rips through the stained-glass windows, shattering every golden chandelier in the hall.

Suffocating, ancient divine gravity slams down from the ceiling. Across the entire hall, three hundred pack warriors, Elders, and High Priest Malakor are slammed violently to their knees, their jaws locked against the flagstones as their inner wolves whimper in terror.

On the high dais, Selene shrieks as she hits the stone on all fours—the stolen *Tear of the Bloodwood* at her throat glitching with sickly pale-green static.

Beside her, Kaelen strains with every muscle in his body, crimson Alpha lightning flaring around his shoulders—before both of his knees slam into the stone floor with a bone-cracking thud.

KAELEN
(straining through clenched teeth)
What... what is this aura?!

**EXT. NORTHERN BORDER ICE-WALL - NIGHT**

Hundreds of miles north, atop a colossal glacier wall under the silver sky, LORD CASSIAN (25)—clad in gunmetal Lycan armor and a frost-wolf pelt cloak—snaps his frost-blue eyes toward the southern horizon. Swirling black frost-mist coils around his gauntlets.

CASSIAN
Three hundred years of winter... The Vessel is awake.

**EXT. BASE OF THE BONE CLIFF - NIGHT**

Standing untouched at the center of the silver crater, Lyra touches the four burning black Vein-Locks on her collarbone, her liquid-silver eyes fixed coldly on Kaelen's palace above.
