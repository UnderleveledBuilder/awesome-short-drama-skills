# EP1 — THE HOUR AND THE DATE

*The hall · Ottilie, Bram, the law-speaker, Marta · roll 214 → 213 · stone —*

**INT. HALL — NIGHT**

Dark. The roll board is the only lit thing in the room.

Two hundred wolves breathing in the dark. We see almost none of them — a shoulder, a hand, the shine of an eye. The sound is the crowd.

**OTTILIE HARROW** stands one pace in front of the dais. Grey wool coat, two winters past its life, sleeves turned. Her cuffs are already straight.

**ALPHA BRAM VOSS** stands one pace behind her. He does not touch her. The distance reads as mercy.

**BRAM**
*(level, kind)*
You are not my mate.

A breath.

**BRAM (CONT'D)**
You are not of this blood. This hall owes you nothing.

Silence.

Something goes out of her chest. One small change in her face. She does not cry. She waits — waits until she is certain her voice will be level.

**OTTILIE**
The fourteenth of the eleventh month. The ninth hour. Write it.

The **LAW-SPEAKER** steps forward. An old man, a book, a pen, a bad knee.

**LAW-SPEAKER**
Child —

**OTTILIE**
In the alpha's record. Write it.

He looks at Bram. Bram says nothing. The law-speaker writes.

She waits. He finishes.

**OTTILIE (CONT'D)**
Read it back.

**LAW-SPEAKER**
The fourteenth of the eleventh month, the ninth hour, the alpha did reject —

**OTTILIE**
All of it.

He reads all of it. She listens to every word.

**OTTILIE (CONT'D)**
Thank you.

She walks to the roll board.

At the front of the hall, **MARTA HARROW** watches with her hands folded in her apron and says nothing.

Ottilie takes the candle off the shelf. She holds the flame to her own name.

The paper catches. The smell of it.

**BRAM**
Ottilie.

She does not turn round.

The name burns through and drops. A hole in the board.

**MOONRISE.**

Outside, the pack's howl goes up and breaks in the middle.

Inside the hall the air moves — a draught from nowhere, as though a door had opened somewhere, and there isn't one.

Nobody moves.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

# EP2 — LET ME STAY

*The boundary stone · Ottilie, Osric · roll 213 · stone 1*

**EXT. BOUNDARY STONE — DUSK**

Wind. No fire. A standing stone, waist high, worn smooth on one face.

**OTTILIE** sits against it with a stick, copying into the dirt: a date, an hour. Slowly. From memory. So that it exists twice.

Footsteps on the path. She does not look up.

**OSRIC BELL** stops. A rope in his hand.

**OSRIC**
The alpha says come home.

**OTTILIE**
*(writing)*
He cut me.

**OSRIC**
He says come home.

**OTTILIE**
He can't take me back. He can't send anyone to fetch me. That's the law you're standing on.

Beat. He looks down at the rope in his hand. He brought a rope and not three wolves because he already knows.

**OSRIC**
I know.

**OTTILIE**
If you stay out here with me, you're not on the roll any more. Three moonrises, and the wolf goes out of you.

**OSRIC**
That's not a thing.

**OTTILIE**
It's written. I'll read it to you.

**OSRIC**
I can't read.

Beat.

He sits down. The wind does the rest.

**OSRIC (CONT'D)**
Nine years I've walked this line. Nobody ever came out here.

Long silence.

**OSRIC (CONT'D)**
Let me stay.

**OTTILIE**
Do you know what you're asking?

**OSRIC**
No.

She looks at him for a long moment. Then she takes a small knife from her coat and cuts a mark into the stone.

**OSRIC (CONT'D)**
What's that?

**OTTILIE**
One.

Something goes through him. Silent. A snap.

He puts his hand flat on the stone. Whatever has been holding him upright since he was fourteen lets go. He does not fall. He does not shift.

**MOONRISE.**

Bram's call rolls out across the territory — the roll called aloud, name after name after name.

Osric's name.

Osric opens his mouth.

Nothing in him answers.

**OTTILIE**
Say your name.

**OSRIC**
What?

**OTTILIE**
Out loud. While it's still yours.

**OSRIC**
*(a beat)*
Osric Bell.

It sounds like a stranger's.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

# EP3 — BRING ME THE ASH

*The boundary stone · Ottilie, Bram, Halloway, Tam · roll 213 → 211 · stone 1 → 3*

**EXT. BOUNDARY STONE — MORNING**

Ottilie sits on the stone.

**BRAM** comes up the path with two wolves behind him. He stops at exactly the distance the law requires — one pace outside the stone.

Behind him: **HALLOWAY**, and her brother **TAM**.

**BRAM**
The pack was wrong to make a spectacle of it.

She waits.

**BRAM (CONT'D)**
The roll will be corrected. You'll be entered as Luna.

Beat.

**BRAM (CONT'D)**
Not as mate. The seat is an office. An office can be given. I have the wording.

**OTTILIE**
You can't put back what I burned.

**BRAM**
I can write it again.

**OTTILIE**
Bring me the ash and I'll consider it.

**BRAM**
You'll starve.

**OTTILIE**
Probably.

Halloway steps out from behind him.

**BRAM (CONT'D)**
*(not turning)*
Not now.

**HALLOWAY**
You promised my household its name if the settlement went clean. The settlement didn't go clean.

**BRAM**
It will.

**HALLOWAY**
You just offered a seat to a girl you threw away. In front of me. So now I know what my name is worth. It's worth whatever you feel like writing.

She turns to Ottilie.

**HALLOWAY (CONT'D)**
I ask to be taken in.

Ottilie looks at her. Then at Bram.

**OTTILIE**
Yes.

**BRAM**
You have no food. No roof. No fire.

**OTTILIE**
No.

**BRAM**
Then why?

**OTTILIE**
Because she asked.

Behind Halloway, Tam says nothing. Ottilie looks at him anyway.

**OTTILIE (CONT'D)**
You too?

Tam glances at his sister. Then he nods.

**OTTILIE (CONT'D)**
Say it.

**TAM**
*(quiet)*
I ask to be taken in.

**OTTILIE**
Yes.

**BRAM**
*(to Halloway)*
Your brother's betrothal is void.

**HALLOWAY**
I did the sum before I stepped out.

Bram goes.

Halloway and Tam do not follow him.

Ottilie takes out the knife. Cuts a mark. Cuts another.

The third one she cuts deeper than the other two, and the knife sticks.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

# EP4 — THE EMPTY CHAIR

*The hall · Ottilie, Neve, the law-speaker, Bram · roll 211 · stone 3*

**INT. HALL — DAY**

She walks in.

Two hundred wolves. The pack does not part for her — it doesn't have to. Nobody can lay a hand on her. She crosses the whole hall and not one of them touches her.

It is the first time in her life the law has done anything for her.

**NEVE CORRIGAN** at the table with a slate, sleeves that fit, hair down.

**NEVE**
*(not looking up)*
Four hundred sacks of grain, not five. Somebody has been counting what they hoped for.

Four chairs. One of them empty.

**LAW-SPEAKER**
The chair is contested.

He opens the book. Puts a finger on the page.

**LAW-SPEAKER (CONT'D)**
The book says: the keeper's chair is held by one who is no wolf, no pack, no mate.

**NEVE**
An office nobody has held for a hundred years, claimed by a girl who cannot shift, on the strength of one page.

**OTTILIE**
Name one wolf in this hall who qualifies.

Silence.

**OTTILIE (CONT'D)**
No wolf. No pack. No mate.

Beat.

**OTTILIE (CONT'D)**
I am the only person in this territory who has ever been poor enough.

She sits down in the empty chair.

The hall does not applaud.

Bram says nothing at all, which is worse than an argument. He reaches over and turns the roll board to the wall.

**NEVE**
*(without looking up from the slate)*
Two hundred and eleven on the roll. Three outside. Stores counted for two hundred and fourteen.

She closes the slate. Looks at Ottilie the way you look at a line item.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

# EP5 — WHAT HAPPENS IN WINTER

*The boundary stone · Ottilie, Osric, Halloway, Rill · roll 211 → 210 · stone 3 → 4*

**EXT. BOUNDARY STONE — NIGHT**

Wind. No fire.

The three of them sleep in the lee of the stone. **TAM** coughs. **HALLOWAY**'s hands are raw from trying to light wet wood.

Footsteps. **OSRIC**, carrying nothing but a knowledge of the ground.

**OSRIC**
There's a cave above the water line. Spring that doesn't freeze. Deer run crosses the boundary a mile east.

**HALLOWAY**
And?

**OSRIC**
And no sentinel walks it now. I've stopped walking it.

**HALLOWAY**
*(bitter)*
You've brought us a map.

**OSRIC**
I've brought you a cave.

Halloway turns on Ottilie.

**HALLOWAY**
They cut our stores. They voided my brother's betrothal. And the woman who took us has got nothing. Not even a fire.

**OTTILIE**
The law doesn't ask me to like you. It asks me to feed you.

She gets up. Takes the knife. Walks out toward the deer run.

Time passes.

She comes back with nothing. She cannot hunt.

Osric takes the knife out of her hand without a word and goes.

Time passes.

He comes back with a deer across his shoulders.

**OTTILIE**
Thank you.

It costs her more than the refusal did.

Footsteps on the path. **RILL** — old, a shawl, three nights on her.

**RILL**
I left bread at the stone this morning. They cut me for it.

**OTTILIE**
Was it said aloud?

**RILL**
In front of the whole hall.

**OTTILIE**
Is it written?

Rill lifts a paper.

**RILL**
They gave me the paper. I can't read it.

Ottilie takes it. Reads it. Then holds it back out.

**OTTILIE**
Ask.

**RILL**
I ask to be taken in.

**OTTILIE**
Yes.

Later.

Rill warms her hands at the fire Osric built. She looks at the stone. At the marks on it.

**RILL**
This is very good.

Beat.

**RILL (CONT'D)**
What happens in winter?

**CUT TO BLACK.**

# EP6 — THE RECORD

*The boundary stone · Ottilie, the law-speaker · roll 210 · stone 4*

**EXT. BOUNDARY STONE — DAWN**

**OTTILIE** sits with her back to the stone and a flat slate on her knees, copying onto it with a stub of chalk: a date, an hour. Slowly. Carefully. A third copy.

Footsteps on the path.

The **LAW-SPEAKER** comes up alone. A satchel, a bad knee, no escort. No escort is the message.

He stops at the lawful distance and sets the satchel down before he speaks, the way a man sets down something he is ashamed of carrying.

**LAW-SPEAKER**
The council has voted. The rejection is to be struck from the record.

She goes on writing.

**LAW-SPEAKER (CONT'D)**
Spoken in haste. That's the wording. It'll be as though it never —

**OTTILIE**
Read it to me first.

**LAW-SPEAKER**
Child, you were there.

**OTTILIE**
Read it.

He opens the book. Finds the page. Reads.

**LAW-SPEAKER**
*You are not my mate. You are not of this blood. This hall owes you nothing.*

He stops. She says nothing. He looks at the page again, and this time he hears it, and his jaw does something.

**OTTILIE**
Again.

He reads it again. Slower. His voice is not steady by the end.

**LAW-SPEAKER**
If I strike it, it never happened. And you can go home.

**OTTILIE**
And if it never happened, I was never thrown.

**LAW-SPEAKER**
Yes.

**OTTILIE**
Then the door stays open.

Beat.

**LAW-SPEAKER**
You understand what you're refusing.

**OTTILIE**
A roof. Yes.

He puts the correction back in the satchel. Buckles it. His hands are not good.

**LAW-SPEAKER**
The council votes on the chair in two days.

**OTTILIE**
I know.

**LAW-SPEAKER**
Do you know what you're doing?

**OTTILIE**
No.

It is the first true thing she has said to an adult in eighteen years. He nods slowly, as though she has passed something.

He goes. She writes his name on the slate, under the date and the hour, in a column she has headed *witness*.

Halfway down the path he stops. He does not turn round.

**LAW-SPEAKER**
I wrote my own name on that page forty years ago.

Beat.

**LAW-SPEAKER (CONT'D)**
It's the only thing I ever wrote.

He goes on down the path.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

# EP7 — THE SLOW GIRL

*The boundary stone · Ottilie, Wren, Halloway · roll 210 · stone 4 → 5*

**EXT. BOUNDARY STONE — DAY**

Rain. Hard, straight, no wind in it.

**WREN ODELL** stands at the stone, soaked through, a coat that was somebody else's, hands hanging. She has walked from the Fennick roll two ridges east since before light, because she did not want to wake anybody.

**OTTILIE** comes out from the lee of the stone.

**OTTILIE**
How long have you been standing there?

**WREN**
A while. I didn't want to be rude.

**OTTILIE**
Were you cut?

**WREN**
For being slow.

She says it flatly, the way you recite a thing you have heard a hundred times.

**WREN (CONT'D)**
Not sick. Not hurt. Not fast enough to be worth the food. That's what he said. In front of the hall.

**OTTILIE**
Was it said aloud?

**WREN**
Yes.

**OTTILIE**
Is it written?

Wren takes a folded paper out of her coat, holds it out. It is sodden and going to pieces.

**WREN**
They gave me this. I can't read it.

Ottilie unfolds it. Reads it. Reads it again.

**OTTILIE**
It's true. It's properly done.

**WREN**
Is that good?

**OTTILIE**
It means the law can reach you.

She turns the paper over and reads the back.

**OTTILIE (CONT'D)**
Three moonrises. If nobody takes you in by the third, the wolf goes out of you. Do you understand what I'm saying?

**WREN**
Yes.

**OTTILIE**
Ask.

**WREN**
I ask to be taken in.

**OTTILIE**
Yes.

Wren does not move. She stands in the rain and looks at her own hands as though they have been given back to her.

**WREN**
What do I do now?

Ottilie opens her mouth and finds she has never once been asked that.

**OTTILIE**
Eat something.

**HALLOWAY** comes round the stone, sees the girl, and does the arithmetic in one look.

**HALLOWAY**
No.

**OTTILIE**
She's asked.

**HALLOWAY**
She can't hunt. She can't carry. She can't run a message in time — look at her. We have four days of deer and a wet fire and you've taken a girl who can't do a single thing.

**OTTILIE**
She can count.

**HALLOWAY**
Anyone can count.

**OTTILIE**
Then it costs us nothing.

Beat. Halloway looks at Wren, who is standing very still, waiting to be decided.

**HALLOWAY**
*(to Wren)*
How many of us are there?

Wren looks round. Counts. Her lips move.

**WREN**
Four.

**HALLOWAY**
Five. You're the fifth.

**WREN**
Five.

She counts again, silently, and gets it right, and is so pleased she forgets to be frightened.

Ottilie takes out the knife and cuts the fifth mark.

Later. Under a lean-to of coats. Wren eating, both cheeks full.

**WREN**
*(mouth full)*
You're all here because you got thrown away.

Halloway stops chewing.

**WREN (CONT'D)**
That's not a home. That's a pile.

She goes back to eating.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

# EP8 — THE PRICE OF A CONVERSATION

*The boundary stone · Ottilie, Rill, Osric · roll 210 · stone 5*

**EXT. BOUNDARY STONE — DUSK**

**RILL** comes up the path with a heel of bread and her shawl pulled tight. **OTTILIE** is cutting at the stone, finishing the fifth mark.

**RILL**
He's put a price on it.

**OTTILIE**
On what?

**RILL**
On talking to you. A wolf who speaks to the keeper forfeits a day's rations. A household that shelters one forfeits three.

She sets the bread down on the stone.

**RILL (CONT'D)**
I left bread here this morning. He had me stand up in the hall so everyone could count me.

**OTTILIE**
Did you say anything?

**RILL**
No.

**OTTILIE**
Why not?

**RILL**
Because saying nothing in front of two hundred people is a thing you can do to a man, if you do it slowly.

Beat.

**OTTILIE**
You shouldn't have brought the bread.

**RILL**
I know.

**OTTILIE**
You'll do it again.

**RILL**
Probably.

A sound on the path. A young wolf — eighteen, nineteen, no bundle, no paper. He stops ten paces off and does not come closer.

He does not speak. He looks at the stone. At the marks. At Ottilie.

Nobody says anything for a long moment. Then he turns and goes back down the path.

**RILL**
He didn't ask.

**OTTILIE**
He came to look at it.

**OSRIC** comes down off the ridge, cloak with the nail in the clasp, and watches the boy go.

**OSRIC**
Another one came at midday. Didn't ask either.

**OTTILIE**
How many?

**OSRIC**
Four since the light went.

**RILL**
*(standing)*
He's going to find out what he's done.

**OTTILIE**
He already knows. That's why he priced it.

**OSRIC**
You know what he's done. He's put a fine on the door.

**OTTILIE**
He's told two hundred wolves exactly where the door is. And he's told them it costs a day's food to knock.

**OSRIC**
He'll work that out by morning.

**OTTILIE**
Yes.

Beat. Osric looks at her.

**OSRIC**
And then what?

**OTTILIE**
Then he'll want names.

Rill pulls her shawl up and turns to go, then stops.

**RILL**
He's going to find out something.

**OTTILIE**
What?

**RILL**
A thing you can't afford is the only thing anybody trusts.

She goes down the path with her empty hands.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

# EP9 — ELEVEN MONTHS

*The boundary stone · Ottilie, Dagny · roll 210 · stone 5*

**EXT. BOUNDARY STONE — MIDDAY**

**DAGNY THORNE** stands in front of the stone, looking at the marks. Not at Ottilie. At the marks.

She has been standing there long enough that the light has moved across her.

The good old coat. The silver braid. Hands that were broken once and set badly.

**OTTILIE** waits. She has learned to wait.

Dagny counts the marks without touching them.

**DAGNY**
Five.

**OTTILIE**
Five.

**DAGNY**
The last one lasted eleven months.

Beat.

**OTTILIE**
How did she die?

**DAGNY**
Ask me a better question.

**OTTILIE**
Who killed her?

**DAGNY**
Better.

Silence. Dagny turns at last and looks at her properly.

**DAGNY**
The council has a seat for a woman who can read. It comes with a roof and a vote and a door that closes behind it.

**OTTILIE**
I've been offered a seat before.

**DAGNY**
Not by me.

**OTTILIE**
You said you'd ask again.

**DAGNY**
I'm asking again.

She does not press. She does not lean on it. She lets it sit in the air like something with weight.

**DAGNY (CONT'D)**
Why did you come yourself?

**OTTILIE**
I didn't.

**DAGNY**
I did. Why did I come myself?

**OTTILIE**
Why did you come yourself?

**DAGNY**
Because I wanted to see whether you'd already started enjoying it.

She turns and walks back down the path.

Halfway she stops. She does not turn round.

**DAGNY (CONT'D)**
She was right about everything.

Beat.

**DAGNY (CONT'D)**
That's what I keep coming back to. She was right, and it didn't save a single one of them.

She goes on down the path and out of sight.

Ottilie stands with the stone behind her and the five marks in it.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

# EP10 — THE WOMAN WHO FOUND HER

*The boundary stone · Ottilie, Marta, Halloway · roll 210 → 209 · stone 5 ·* ⭐ **PAYWALL**

**EXT. BOUNDARY STONE — THIRD NIGHT**

No fire. The fire is out and nobody has relit it.

**MARTA HARROW** comes up the path with a bundle, an apron, and a house key hanging from her belt — a key she no longer has a door for.

She has gone grey at the front since that morning.

**OTTILIE** is sitting on the stone. She does not get up.

**MARTA**
You've not eaten. Don't tell me you've eaten.

**OTTILIE**
I've eaten.

**MARTA**
You haven't.

She stops at the lawful distance. She looks at the stone, and the marks, and the ground, and then at the girl.

**MARTA (CONT'D)**
They cut me today. My own household. They voted on it in the kitchen and I was standing there. They said they wouldn't be asked what I did.

**OTTILIE**
What did you do?

**MARTA**
You know what I did.

**OTTILIE**
Say it.

Marta's hands find her apron and hold it.

**MARTA**
On your sixteenth birthday I petitioned the council. To strike your name off the roll. And re-enter you as unblooded servant, so the household wouldn't be shamed.

Beat.

**MARTA (CONT'D)**
I did it so we'd both be safe.

**OTTILIE**
Say the rest.

**MARTA**
I've practised it. Every night for two years, I've practised saying it to you. I had it better than that.

**OTTILIE**
Then say it properly.

**MARTA**
I made you smaller so we would both be safe.

Silence. Ottilie's voice does not change at all.

**OTTILIE**
Were you cut?

**MARTA**
Yes.

**OTTILIE**
Was it said aloud?

**MARTA**
In front of the whole household.

**OTTILIE**
Is it written?

Marta reaches into her apron and takes out a paper, folded small, worn soft at the creases. She has been holding it all day.

She holds it out. Ottilie does not take it.

**OTTILIE**
Read it.

**MARTA**
I can't.

**OTTILIE**
Then it's not written, and the law can't reach you, and you can go home.

Beat. Marta looks at the paper in her own hand.

**MARTA**
I can't go home. That's the whole of it.

Long silence. The wind. The dark behind her.

**MARTA (CONT'D)**
I ask to be taken in.

Beat.

**MARTA (CONT'D)**
*(quieter)*
I'm sorry. I know that isn't the form.

Ottilie straightens her cuffs.

Behind her, **HALLOWAY** steps out of the dark.

**HALLOWAY**
Don't.

Ottilie does not turn round. She looks past Marta, at the row of faces behind her — Halloway, Tam, Wren, Osric, Rill — all of them standing where they can see, all of them waiting to find out what she is.

She opens her mouth.

**CUT TO BLACK.**
